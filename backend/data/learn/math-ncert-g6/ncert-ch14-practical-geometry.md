**Practical geometry** is about **constructing** accurate figures using a **ruler** and **compasses** — not just drawing freehand.

## Tools

| Tool | Use |
|------|-----|
| **Ruler** (straight edge) | Draw straight segments; measure length |
| **Compasses** | Draw arcs and circles; mark equal lengths |
| **Divider** | Compare lengths |
| **Set-squares** | 30°–60°–90° and 45°–45°–90°; draw perpendicular and parallel lines |
| **Protractor** | Measure and draw angles |

In classical constructions: ruler **only** for lines, compasses **only** for arcs — not for measuring off distances like a ruler.

## Constructing a circle

A **circle** is all points at a fixed distance (**radius**) from a **centre**.

**Circle of radius 3 cm:**
1. Mark centre O
2. Open compasses to 3 cm
3. Place point at O, draw the full turn

## Constructing a line segment of given length

**Segment AB = 4.5 cm:**
1. Draw a starting line with the ruler
2. Mark A
3. Measure 4.5 cm with ruler or compasses
4. Mark B

## Copying an angle

To copy ∠X at point P:
1. Draw an arc cutting both arms of ∠X
2. With same opening, draw an arc from P
3. Mark the same arc length on the new arc
4. Join P to that mark

## Perpendicular to a line through a point

Use ruler + set-square, or compasses:
- From a point **on** the line: equal arcs on both sides of the point, then join
- From a point **off** the line: arcs from the point cutting the line, then standard perpendicular construction

## Perpendicular bisector of a segment

1. Open compasses to more than half of AB
2. Draw arcs above and below from A and from B
3. Join the two intersection points — this line bisects AB at 90°

Every point on the perpendicular bisector is **equidistant** from A and B.

## Constructing angles (60°, 90°, 120°)

- **60°:** construct an equilateral triangle (all arcs same radius)
- **90°:** perpendicular to a line
- **120°:** 60° + another 60° adjacent

Practice with **light marks** and **sharp** pencil/compass points for clean figures.
