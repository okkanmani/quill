A **fraction** represents **part of a whole** or **part of a group**. The parts must be **equal**.

**Example:** 3 out of 4 equal parts of a poori → **3/4**

## Parts of a fraction

In **5/12**:
- **Numerator (5)** — how many equal parts are taken
- **Denominator (12)** — into how many equal parts the whole is divided

## Types of fractions

| Type | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Proper** | Numerator < denominator | 3/7, 2/5 |
| **Improper** | Numerator ≥ denominator | 7/4, 5/5 |
| **Mixed** | Whole number + proper fraction | 2 1/3 |

**Improper to mixed:** 7/4 = 1 3/4 (7 ÷ 4 = 1 remainder 3)

## Equivalent fractions

Same value, different look — multiply or divide **top and bottom by the same number**:

**1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6**

Use equivalents to **compare** or **add** fractions with different denominators.

## Simplest form

Divide numerator and denominator by their **HCF** until no common factor remains.

**8/12 = 2/3**

## Comparing fractions

- Same denominator → larger numerator wins: 5/8 > 3/8
- Same numerator → smaller denominator wins: 1/3 > 1/5
- Otherwise → convert to equivalent fractions with a **common denominator**

## Addition and subtraction

**Same denominator:** add/subtract numerators; keep denominator.

**3/7 + 2/7 = 5/7**

**Different denominators:** find equivalent fractions with the **LCM** as common denominator, then add or subtract.

## Fractions on the number line

Between 0 and 1, divide the unit into equal parts (denominator), then count parts (numerator).

**3/4** is three steps to the right of 0 when the unit is split into fourths.

## Fraction of a number

**1/4 of 20** = 20 ÷ 4 = **5**  
**3/5 of 30** = (30 ÷ 5) × 3 = **18**
