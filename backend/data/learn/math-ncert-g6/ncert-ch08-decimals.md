**Decimals** are another way to write numbers — especially useful for **money**, **measurements**, and **parts of a whole** when the denominator is 10, 100, etc.

**₹5.75** means 5 rupees and 75 paise (75 hundredths of a rupee).

## Tenths and hundredths

| Fraction | Decimal | Read as |
|----------|---------|---------|
| 1/10 | 0.1 | one tenth |
| 3/10 | 0.3 | three tenths |
| 1/100 | 0.01 | one hundredth |
| 25/100 | 0.25 | twenty-five hundredths |

**10 mm = 1 cm** → 5 mm = 0.5 cm  
Pencil 7 cm 5 mm = **7.5 cm** · 8 cm 3 mm = **8.3 cm**

## Place value

| Hundreds | Tens | Ones | . | Tenths | Hundredths |
|----------|------|------|---|--------|------------|
| 100 | 10 | 1 | · | 1/10 | 1/100 |

**23.5** = 2 tens + 3 ones + 5 tenths  
**602.8** = 6 hundreds + 2 ones + 8 tenths (no tens)

## Decimals on the number line

Between 0 and 1, split the unit into **10 equal parts** for tenths.

**0.6** is six steps right of 0.  
**2.3** is two full units plus three tenths past 2.

## Comparing decimals

Compare **whole parts** first; if equal, compare **tenths**, then **hundredths**.

- 2.45 > 2.4 (same ones and tenths; 5 hundredths > 0)
- 3.08 < 3.15

## Addition and subtraction

Line up the **decimal points** and add/subtract as with whole numbers.

```
  12.5
+  7.3
------
  19.8
```

## Converting between fractions and decimals

- **Tenths:** 7/10 = 0.7
- **Hundredths:** 45/100 = 0.45
- **Mixed:** 2 + 3/10 = 2.3

## Money

100 paise = ₹1 → 75 paise = **₹0.75** · 50 paise = **₹0.50**
