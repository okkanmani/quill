**Mensuration** is about measuring the **boundary** and **region** of plane figures — how much fence is needed, or how much surface a shape covers.

## Perimeter

**Perimeter** is the distance around a **closed** figure — one full trip along the boundary.

| Shape | Perimeter |
|-------|-----------|
| Rectangle (l × b) | 2 × (l + b) |
| Square (side s) | 4 × s |
| Triangle | sum of all three sides |
| General polygon | sum of all side lengths |

**Example:** Rectangle 40 cm × 10 cm → 2 × (40 + 10) = **100 cm**

**Regular polygon:** all sides equal → perimeter = (number of sides) × (length of one side)  
Regular hexagon, perimeter 18 cm → one side = 18 ÷ 6 = **3 cm**

## Area

**Area** is the **amount of region** inside a closed figure — measured in square units (cm², m²).

| Shape | Area |
|-------|------|
| Rectangle | length × breadth |
| Square | side × side = s² |

**Example:** Rectangle 6 cm × 4 cm → area = **24 cm²**

**Square units:** a 1 cm × 1 cm tile covers **1 cm²**.

## Perimeter vs area

- Same perimeter → **different** shapes can have **different** areas
- Fencing a field → **perimeter**
- Painting a wall or tiling a floor → **area**

## Units

| Length | Area |
|--------|------|
| cm, m, km | cm², m² |
| 1 m = 100 cm | 1 m² = 10,000 cm² |

Convert carefully: **1 m² is not 100 cm²** — it is 100 × 100 = 10,000 cm².

## Practical uses

- **Perimeter:** framing a photo, fencing land, running track lap distance
- **Area:** carpet for a room, paint for a wall, land for planting
